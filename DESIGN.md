# Zeroed design direction

Recorded from the product owner's answers (Solvra Development). Agents treat this file as design data.

- **Name:** Zeroed. "Zeroing" a sight means calibrating it so it hits exactly where you aim.
- **Maker credit:** Developed and maintained by Solvra Development.
- **Look and feel (owner's pick: Tactical / HUD):** military-instrument feel. Sharp corners, tight spacing,
  one hot signal colour, readable at a glance. Should sit naturally next to shooters such as Blackhawk Rescue Mission 5.
- **Logo (owner's pick: letter mark):** a monogram built from the letter Z, with a reticle detail.
